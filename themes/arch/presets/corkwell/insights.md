# Corkwell

## Suits

A wine bar: natural wines, cocktails and small plates in a room people linger in. The site sells atmosphere first and information second. `index` opens on a `video-popup` (a still with a play button) followed by a `rich-text` headline, a `checkerboard` of three reasons to stay, a `priced-list` of what is pouring set on a dark band, an `image-callout` about the room and a closing `action-bar` with opening days. `menu` is `priced-list` sections for cocktails, plates and cheese, with a `content-switcher` that flips between wine-list categories; `events` is where this preset differs from a cafe: a `countdown` to the next tasting, an `icon-list` of weekly regulars and an `image-callout` for private hire. `gallery` is two `gallery` sections (what we pour, inside the bar); `about` has the owners' story, an `icon-list` of principles, a `profile-grid` and a producers `image-text`; `contact` is a `map` and an `accordion` of practical questions.

It seeds the `news` collection with five posts (tastings, a new flight, how to read the list, staff intro). Prices are display only; there is no reservation, ticketing or shop, and private hire is an email request.

## Look

Dim, intimate and a little literary: a candlelit room rather than a bright cafe. Light surfaces are soft stone rather than white, highlight bands are a deep blue-black, and the accent is a dark teal (sea-green on dark), a rare cool palette for hospitality. Cormorant Garamond at a light weight gives tall, elegant headings; Space Grotesk body text keeps it contemporary rather than old-world. Corners and buttons are `sharp`; `spacing_density` is `compact`, so sections sit close like a dense menu card. The header is `transparent_on_hero` in the dark `highlight-primary` scheme over the video opener, not sticky, with empty contact lines; inner pages open on `highlight-primary` `banner` titles.

## Adapting

- Cocktail bar or speakeasy: drop the `content-switcher` wine list for a second cocktail `priced-list`, keep `events` and the `countdown` for guest-bartender nights.
- Bistro or small-plates restaurant: promote food on `menu`, keep the drinks `priced-list` on `index`, turn private hire into group bookings copy.
- Wine shop with tastings: `news` becomes new arrivals, the `content-switcher` becomes shelves by region, but remember nothing is sold online.
- Jazz club or live-music venue: `events` carries the programme with `event-list` instead of `icon-list`, `gallery` becomes the stage.
- Poor fit for bright, family-daytime businesses (bakery, brunch, kids' cafe): the palette and `compact` rhythm are built for evening mood. Poor fit for a multi-venue group or anything needing table reservations or ticket sales.

# Crumbly

## Suits

An artisan bakery with a custom-cake side: a walk-in shop whose website shows what is in the case, tempts people with photography and funnels special-occasion orders. `index` opens on a `slideshow`, then a `trust-bar` of freshness claims, `sliding-panels` of product families, an `image-callout` pushing custom cakes, a `testimonial-hero`, a `project-showcase` of recent bakes and a closing `action-bar`. `menu` is four `priced-list` sections (bread, pastries, cakes, cookies) plus a made-to-order `image-text`. `custom-cakes` is the conversion page: `numbered-cards` for the ordering process, a `comparison-table` of cake tiers, a `gallery` of past cakes and an `action-bar`. `gallery` adds a `bento-grid`, a behind-the-counter `gallery` and an `event-list` of classes; `about` has the origin `image-text`, `features-split`, `key-figures` and a `profile-grid` of bakers; `contact` is a `map` with an `image-callout` for cake enquiries.

It seeds the `news` collection with five posts (seasonal buns, daily sourdough, the 4 a.m. shift, a donation milestone). Suitability is not functionality: "Order a Cake" leads to `custom-cakes`, where the order is placed by phone or email; the `comparison-table` compares, it does not sell, and classes on the `event-list` are not ticketed.

## Look

Warm, homely and a bit playful without being twee. Light surfaces are warm off-white with a toasted secondary band, but the highlight bands are a deep plum-aubergine and the accent a dusty mauve (pale lilac on dark), which keeps it away from the usual brown-and-kraft bakery look. Fraunces headings are soft and slightly quirky; Nunito Sans body is rounded and friendly. Corners are `rounded`, buttons follow with `button_shape` on `auto`, spacing is `default`. The header is solid (`transparent_on_hero` off), sticky, with opening hours as its one contact line next to the menu; inner pages open on `highlight-primary` `banner` titles except `contact`, which starts with `rich-text`.

## Adapting

- Patisserie or chocolatier: rename the `priced-list` sections, keep `custom-cakes` as bespoke orders, let the `bento-grid` carry the hero photography.
- Cake designer working from home: drop `menu`, make `custom-cakes` the landing page, keep the `comparison-table` of tiers and the `gallery`.
- Deli, cheesemonger or farm shop: `sliding-panels` become counters, `event-list` becomes tastings, `news` becomes seasonal arrivals.
- Cooking school: the `event-list` on `gallery` moves to its own page with `class-schedule`, `numbered-cards` describe how to book.
- Poor fit for restaurants with table service (no reservations, no evening mood) and for a franchise or chain: one shop, one `map`, one family of bakers is the whole story.

# Torque

## Suits

- An independent auto repair shop positioning itself against the dealership on price transparency and respect for the customer's time. The `index` page makes that case at length: a `large` photo `banner`, a `trust-bar` of certifications, a `card-grid` of service areas, a `features-split` on time and billing, an `image-text` on the workshop, a `highlight-secondary` `key-figures` band, `testimonials` in a `carousel`, an `image-callout` about photo reports and a closing `action-bar`.
- `services` is priced before you arrive: a `checkerboard` of four service areas, a `priced-list` of common jobs, a `comparison-table` of inspection levels and an `accordion`. `specials` is the offers page, with a `card-grid` of current deals, a `highlight-secondary` `countdown` to a deadline and a `priced-list` of bundles. Prices and offers are displayed only; nothing is booked, paid or redeemed on the site.
- `about` leans on longevity and credentials with a `timeline` and a `profile-grid` of certified technicians. `contact` pairs `contact-details`, a `map` and an `accordion` of drop-off questions.
- Seeds `news` with five posts used as seasonal checks, a dashboard-light explainer and workshop stories. No `projects` or `services` items.
- Every page ends on a `highlight-primary` `action-bar` pushing to a call or a visit; the header repeats the phone number and certification line.

## Look

Industrial and energetic: cool grey-white surfaces, a vivid orange accent, and deep charcoal-navy highlight bands where the orange brightens. Both fonts are sans serif: a bold geometric heading face with a technical edge (Space Grotesk) over a neutral body (Inter), which reads as modern workshop rather than old garage. The header is `sticky`, `transparent_on_hero` with `center_nav`, not `full_width`, floating over the home `banner`; every inner page opens with a `small` photo `banner`. `spacing_density` is `default`, corners `slightly-rounded`, buttons `auto`: dense, structured pages with many sections, suited to a visitor comparing prices.

## Adapting

- Tyre and exhaust centre, body shop or detailing studio: keep the structure; the `checkerboard` and `priced-list` carry any service menu, and `specials` with its `countdown` suits seasonal tyre or detailing deals.
- Motorcycle or bicycle workshop: same pages, smaller `priced-list`; the `comparison-table` compares service levels.
- Appliance, phone or computer repair: swap the `trust-bar` certifications, keep the price-before-work promise on `services` and the `specials` page for trade-in or bundle offers.
- Boat, caravan or small-engine service: keep the workshop `image-text` and the `profile-grid`; use `news` for seasonal lay-up and recommissioning checks.
- Poor fit for a car dealership or any sales-led business: there is no inventory and the voice is explicitly anti-dealer. Also a poor fit for a premium, image-led brand, since the pages are busy with figures, tables and offers rather than photography.

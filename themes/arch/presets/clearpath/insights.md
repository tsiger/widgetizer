# Clearpath

## Suits

An independent consultant or very small advisory practice: one named expert selling strategy and operations engagements to growth-stage companies, one at a time. The site is text-led and reputation-led. `index` opens on a full-width `image` followed by a `split-content` statement of what the practice is, then a `logo-cloud` of past clients, a `numbered-service-list` describing the three phases of an engagement, a `split-content` featured engagement, a `testimonial-hero` and a closing `banner` invitation. `approach` explains the method with a `checkerboard` and an `accordion` of pre-engagement questions; `work` tells three case studies as alternating `image-text` sections rather than a grid; `about` carries a personal `rich-text` manifesto, a career `timeline` and a `trust-bar` of credentials; `contact` puts `contact-details` on a dark band with an `accordion` of what to say in a first note.

It seeds the `news` collection with five short posts (planning clinics, a metric essay, an anniversary note) for `news`. Nothing is booked or sold: the header button "Start a conversation" points at `contact`, which lists an email and phone.

## Look

Quiet, editorial and intellectual, more essay than brochure. White primary surfaces with a cool lavender-grey secondary band, very dark ink-navy highlight bands and an electric indigo accent (periwinkle on dark) that is the one vivid note. Fraunces at a light weight for headings against Inter Tight body gives a literary, slightly academic pairing. Corners and buttons are `sharp`, and `spacing_density` is `airy`, so pages breathe and read slowly. The header is solid (`transparent_on_hero` off), sticky, with email and phone as contact lines; every inner page opens with a `split-content` title rather than a photo banner, so the whole site feels typeset rather than designed around imagery.

## Adapting

- Executive coach or leadership advisor: rename `approach` to How I work, turn the `numbered-service-list` phases into coaching formats, keep the `timeline` biography.
- Fractional CFO, COO or marketing lead: the three `image-text` cases on `work` become client engagements; add `pricing` for retainers if you publish them.
- Author, speaker or researcher: `news` becomes essays, `logo-cloud` becomes publications or stages, and the `checkerboard` on `approach` becomes talks or books.
- Boutique law or tax advisory (one partner): keep the credentials `trust-bar`, replace the `work` cases with practice areas in an `icon-card-grid`.
- Poor fit for a team-based firm (there is no `profile-grid` and the voice is first-person singular) and for anything visual-first: with only one real photo opener and text openers everywhere else, a photographer, restaurant or shop would feel starved of imagery.

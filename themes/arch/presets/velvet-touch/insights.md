# Velvet Touch

## Suits

- A boutique day spa or massage studio where the sale is atmosphere first and treatments second. The `index` page is sensory and short: a `split-hero` with a photo on `highlight-primary`, a `trust-bar`, a `sliding-panels` section to explore the offer, an `image-callout` on touch, a `testimonial-slider` and an `action-bar` inviting the visitor to unwind.
- `services` is the practical page: a `card-grid` of what is offered, a `highlight-primary` `trust-bar`, an `image-text` on what to expect and a `pricing` grid of treatment packages. `treatments` is the long-form companion, three alternating `image-text` sections for the signature treatments and a `highlight-primary` `image-callout` on combining them. The `services` collection is seeded with five treatments so each has a detail page.
- Suitability is not functionality: the header invites visitors to book and the `contact` page is titled around booking, but no appointment is reserved or paid for on the site; the phone number and email in the footer are the route in.
- `about` introduces the therapists with a `profile-grid` and the space with a `gallery`; `gallery` and `contact` are simple supporting pages with a `map` and an `accordion`.
- Seeds `news` with five posts used as new-treatment announcements, seasonal packages and skincare tips. No `projects` items.

## Look

Hushed, warm and luxurious: blush-white surfaces, a muted mauve-rose accent, and deep plum-brown highlight bands where the accent becomes a dusty pink. The type is the most fashion-forward in the catalogue: a high-contrast Didone serif at regular weight (Bodoni Moda) over a soft humanist sans (Lato). The header is `sticky`, `full_width`, sits on `standard-secondary` with a `secondary` style call to action and `transparent_on_hero` off, so it is a solid, quiet bar everywhere; the home page opens with a `split-hero` and inner pages with a `small` photo `banner`. `spacing_density` is `default`, corners `slightly-rounded`, buttons `auto`: polished and restrained rather than airy.

## Adapting

- Beauty salon, facial clinic or aesthetics practice: keep the structure; rewrite `treatments` for the signature procedures and the `pricing` packages for courses of sessions.
- Massage therapy or physiotherapy practice: `treatments` becomes modalities, the `trust-bar` carries qualifications, and `news` becomes self-care advice.
- Boutique hotel spa or wellness retreat: `treatments` becomes experiences, `gallery` grows, and the `sliding-panels` on `index` introduce rooms or packages.
- Jeweller, perfumer or luxury gift shop: the hushed palette and Didone headings suit a premium brand; `treatments` becomes collections and `pricing` becomes price tiers, though nothing is sold online.
- Poor fit for a busy, high-volume salon that competes on price and speed, since the pages are slow and image-led with few prices, and for any brand that wants bright, energetic colour; the palette is deliberately muted and the mood is quiet.

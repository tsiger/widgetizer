# Inkwell

## Suits

- Appointment-based creative studios where the artists are the product: tattoo studios first, then piercing, permanent make-up, barbershops with named chairs, small photography collectives. The home is portfolio and people: a dark photo `banner`, a `scrolling-text` strip of styles, `sliding-panels` introducing the residents, a large `masonry-gallery`, an `image-callout` on how a custom piece is made and a `bento-grid` of the space.
- The `artists` page is a stack of fused `split-content` sections, one per artist with a photo, bio and button, closed by a `testimonials` carousel. The `work` page is pure gallery (`checkerboard` of favourite pieces, then a `masonry-gallery`). `visit` and `contact` explain the process in `image-callout`, `accordion` and a numbered-list `split-content`; there is no price list anywhere, by design.
- Seeds `news` (flash days, aftercare, new residents). No `projects` or `services`.
- Suitability is not functionality: "Book a consult" and every `action-bar` go to `contact`, which tells the visitor what to send by email; nothing is booked or paid on the site.

## Look

Urban, graphic and high-contrast. Cool light-grey surfaces (not white), near-black and pure-black highlight bands, a deep blood-red accent that brightens to scarlet on dark surfaces. Syne headings, bold and wide, over IBM Plex Sans body text: a display sans with attitude paired with a neutral workhorse. Everything is `sharp` (corners and buttons), default spacing, `full_width` header. The header is `transparent_on_hero` but only the home opens with a photo `banner`; every inner page opens with a text-led `split-content` so the header stays readable on light surfaces. Highlights carry the opener, one proof or process band mid-page and the closing `action-bar`, with `highlight-secondary` used once on the home for variety.

## Adapting

- Barbershop or hair studio with named stylists: `artists` becomes the team, `work` the cuts, `visit` stays; add a `pricing` or `priced-list` section to `visit` if prices are public.
- Photography or design collective: `artists` as members, `work` as the portfolio (consider seeding `projects` and switching the `work` gallery to a `project-showcase`), `visit` becomes the studio page.
- Piercing or permanent make-up studio: keep everything; the `accordion` healing and consent questions map directly.
- Music or recording studio: `artists` becomes engineers and producers, `work` a discography or client list, the `bento-grid` the rooms.
- Poor fit for a bright, friendly or family-facing brand: the dark palette and sharp display type are deliberately edgy. Also a poor fit for any business that wants prices or packages up front; the preset has no pricing section and the process pages are written around consultation first.

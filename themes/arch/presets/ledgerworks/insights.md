# Ledgerworks

## Suits

- Advisory practices that sell ongoing, explained expertise to small businesses: accountants and bookkeepers first, then tax advisers, payroll bureaus, business consultants and compliance firms. The site is text-led and process-led rather than image-led.
- The home opens with a `split-hero` (headline beside one photo), then a `trust-bar`, a `numbered-service-list` of practice areas, a `content-switcher` that re-pitches the firm for different kinds of client, `steps` from first call to ongoing partnership, a `testimonial-hero` on a `highlight-primary` band and an `accordion` before the closing `action-bar`.
- `services` combines a `numbered-service-list` with a three-tier `pricing` section (flat fees) and an expectations `accordion`. `industries` is an `icon-card-grid` of specialties with a `features-split` and `testimonials`. `team` is `profile-grid`, `key-figures`, `timeline` and credentials `trust-bar`. `resources` is the standout: a `timeline` of key deadlines, a long `accordion` and a `resource-list` of downloadable checklists.
- Seeds `news` (service launches, year-end evenings, tips). No `projects` or `services` collection; the service list is written into the pages.
- Suitability is not functionality: "Book a consultation" and every `action-bar` open `contact`, which is a `split-content` invitation, a `map` and an `accordion`; no scheduler, intake form or client portal. The `resource-list` links to files the author uploads.

## Look

Sober, warm and bookish. Paper-coloured off-white and pale linen surfaces, deep forest-green `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands, a dark green accent that becomes soft sage on dark. Literata headings (a text serif made for reading) over Inter Tight body text: literate and precise rather than grand. `sharp` corners, default spacing. The header is solid (`transparent_on_hero` off), `full_width` and `sticky`, with the phone number and office hours placed in the menu area. Every inner page opens with a plain `rich-text` title block, so there are no photo banners after the home; emphasis comes from one dark testimonial or proof band per page and the closing call to action.

## Adapting

- Law firm or notary: rename `industries` to "Practice areas" and `resources` to "Guides"; keep the deadline `timeline` for statutory dates and drop `pricing` if fees are not published.
- Business, management or marketing consultancy: `industries` stays, `services` becomes engagements, `pricing` becomes retainers; turn `news` into insights.
- IT support or managed services: the `content-switcher` becomes "By company size", `resources` becomes a knowledge base, the `pricing` tiers map directly onto support plans.
- Poor fit for image-led or lifestyle businesses (hospitality, trades, fitness): there is no gallery, slideshow or photo opener beyond the home, and the serif voice is deliberately quiet. Also a poor fit if the business refuses to show prices or process; two of its best sections depend on them.

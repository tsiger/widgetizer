# Brewline

## Suits

A neighbourhood coffee shop or small roastery cafe: a walk-in hospitality business whose website exists to get people through the door, show the menu and keep regulars informed. `index` opens on a dark `split-hero` (photo beside headline), runs a `scrolling-text` marquee, an `image-callout` about sourcing, `image-tabs` that let visitors browse drink families, a `testimonial-slider` of regulars and a closing `action-bar`. `menu` is three `priced-list` sections (coffee, seasonal, pastries) plus a take-home `image-text`; `gallery` pairs a `gallery` with a `masonry-gallery`; `about` tells the origin story with `image-text`, a `steps` brewing method, `key-figures` and `team-highlight`; `contact` is a `map`, a `schedule-table` of hours and a short `accordion`.

It seeds the `news` collection with five posts (new origins, cupping evenings, staff intros, a new roaster partner) shown on `news`. Prices on the menu are display only: nothing is ordered or paid online, and the header's "View Menu" button simply links to `menu`.

## Look

Warm, friendly and a little craft-market. Pure white primary surfaces with a warm oat-coloured secondary band, near-black `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands, and a forest-green accent (mint-green on dark). Lora, a readable humanist serif, heads it; DM Sans keeps body text soft and modern. Corners are `rounded` and buttons are `pill`, the friendliest shape set in the catalogue. The header is `transparent_on_hero`, sticky, with contact lines beside the menu; inner pages open on `highlight-primary` `banner` titles except `contact`, which starts with `rich-text`. Spacing is `default`.

## Adapting

- Tea house, juice bar or ice-cream parlour: rename the `priced-list` sections, swap the brewing `steps` for how you make it, keep the `image-tabs` as flavour families.
- Bakery-cafe or brunch spot: add a fourth `priced-list` for food, turn the `scrolling-text` into opening-hours or specials, keep `schedule-table`.
- Small roaster with wholesale: turn `news` into origin notes and roast releases, add an `image-text` on `menu` for wholesale enquiries.
- Craft brewery taproom or cocktail bar: darker photography does most of the work; `image-tabs` become beer or cocktail styles and `about` keeps the process `steps`.
- Poor fit for a multi-location chain (one `map`, one hours table, one voice) and for anything that needs online ordering or table reservations; the menu is a list, not a shop.

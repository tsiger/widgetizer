# Keystoned

## Suits

- A real estate agent or small brokerage, and anyone who sells a few high-value things one at a time with a personal relationship: property developers, holiday-home agents, relocation consultants. The home opens with a photo `slideshow`, then a `trust-bar`, a `card-grid` of featured properties, an `image-text` about the agent, `testimonials` and an `icon-card-grid` splitting buyers from sellers.
- `properties` is a `project-showcase` of listings with `key-figures` for recent results, and `property-details` is a worked example of a single listing page (fact `key-figures`, `image-text` description, `gallery`, `icon-card-grid` of features, `map`); copy it for each listing. `neighborhoods` is a `card-grid` of areas with an `image-callout`; `guides` ("Buyers & Sellers") is two `image-text` sections and a `steps` walk from first call to closing.
- `about` opens with an `image-text` on a `highlight-primary` band and leans on `key-figures` and a `testimonial-slider`; `contact` opens with a `features-split` of ways to get in touch, then `map` and `accordion`.
- Seeds `news` (market days, viewings, community). Listings are pages, not a collection.
- Suitability is not functionality: "View Properties" is a link, not a search. There is no listings feed, filter, valuation tool or enquiry form; every property is a hand-built page kept current by the author.

## Look

Polished and residential. Warm plaster and parchment surfaces, near-black `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands, an oxblood accent that lifts to dusty rose on dark surfaces. Cinzel headings (an engraved Roman capital face) over Barlow body text: heritage signage paired with a plain, slightly condensed sans. `sharp` corners but `pill` buttons, and an `airy` spacing density that gives listings room to breathe. The `full_width` header is `transparent_on_hero` over the home `slideshow` and the photo `banner` on `properties`, `neighborhoods` and `property-details`; `about`, `guides` and `contact` open with content sections instead. Highlights appear as the closing `action-bar` on every page and one mid-page emphasis.

## Adapting

- Property developer or new-build scheme: `properties` becomes the schemes, `property-details` the single development, `neighborhoods` the locations; use `key-figures` for completion dates and unit counts.
- Holiday-let or serviced-apartment agency: `property-details` becomes the rental page, `guides` becomes "Owners & Guests"; it still takes no bookings.
- Interior designer or home stager: `properties` becomes the portfolio (consider seeding `projects` for the `project-showcase`), `neighborhoods` is dropped, `guides` becomes the process.
- Poor fit for a large agency with hundreds of live listings; each one is a page, and nothing syncs with a listings system. Also a poor fit for brands that want to feel modern-tech or playful; the Roman capitals and oxblood accent read as traditional and upscale.

# Olympic

## Suits

- Membership venues with a timetable: gyms first, then CrossFit boxes, climbing walls, martial-arts academies, swim and leisure centres, yoga or dance studios with many weekly classes. The structure assumes several coaches, a weekly schedule and tiered memberships.
- The home opens with a large photo `banner`, a `trust-bar`, a `card-grid` of training styles, an `image-text`, `key-figures` on a `highlight-primary` band and member `testimonials`, closing on a `highlight-secondary` `action-bar`. `classes` is the operational page: an `icon-card-grid` of class types and a tabbed `class-schedule` with the full weekly timetable. `pricing` is the decision page: three `pricing` plans, an `icon-card-grid` of what every membership includes and an `accordion` of common questions.
- `about` carries values (`steps`), coaches (`profile-grid`) and `testimonials`; `contact` is a `map` with opening hours and an `accordion`.
- Seeds `services` (membership, personal training, classes, assessment, 24-hour access) and `news` (new equipment, induction weeks, training tips).
- Suitability is not functionality: "Start Free Trial" is a link to `pricing`, not a sign-up; there is no membership checkout, class booking or member login. The `class-schedule` is a hand-maintained timetable, not a live booking grid.

## Look

Loud, clean and athletic. Pure white and cool grey standard surfaces, deep navy `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands, and a hot orange accent that is the only warm colour on the page. Barlow Condensed headings, bold and tall, over Inter body text: sports-jersey headlines with a neutral, legible sans. `sharp` corners and buttons, default spacing. The `full_width` header is `transparent_on_hero` and every page, including `contact`, opens with a photo `banner`, so the header sits over an image everywhere. The home uses both highlight surfaces (figures mid-page, `action-bar` at the end); inner pages reserve `highlight-primary` for the opener and the closing call to action.

## Adapting

- Climbing gym, boxing club or martial-arts academy: keep everything; rewrite `services` items as membership and class formats and re-enter the `class-schedule`.
- Yoga, pilates or dance studio: soften the headlines, keep the timetable and `pricing`; replace `key-figures` with an `image-text` on the space, and turn `news` into workshops and retreats.
- Sports club (football, tennis, athletics): `classes` becomes training sessions, `pricing` becomes membership tiers, `profile-grid` the coaching staff; `services` can hold the club's offers.
- Poor fit for a solo trainer or coach: `class-schedule`, `pricing` tiers and a coaches `profile-grid` all presume scale, and the voice is institutional rather than personal. Also a poor fit for calm, premium or wellness-spa brands; the orange-and-navy palette and condensed type shout.

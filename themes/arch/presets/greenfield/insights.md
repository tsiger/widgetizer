# Greenfield

## Suits

- Trade and outdoor service businesses whose work is visible: landscaping, garden design, paving and decking, tree care, pool and irrigation contractors. The story is "look at what we built": the home runs a photo `slideshow`, a before-and-after `comparison-slider`, homeowner `testimonials` and a quote-request `action-bar`, and the `portfolio` page is a `project-showcase` fed by the `projects` collection.
- The `services` page is a `card-grid` of offerings followed by `steps` that walk a client from idea to finished work, so it fits any business that sells a project with a process rather than a one-off purchase.
- The `about` page is crew-led (`profile-grid`, `accordion`); the `contact` page is a `map` plus a "before you call" `accordion`, which fits a business reached by phone and site visit.
- Seeds `projects` (finished landscapes) and `news` (crew updates, seasonal tips, open mornings). Both are meant to grow with the business.
- Suitability is not functionality: the "Free Estimate" header button and the closing `action-bar` link to the `contact` page; nothing books or quotes automatically, and the site collects no form data.

## Look

Fresh and outdoorsy. Near-white and pale sage surfaces, deep forest-green highlight bands (`highlight-primary`, `highlight-secondary`), a leaf-green accent and an amber rating star; the only warmth comes from the photos. Headings in Lexend, body in Cabin: two friendly geometric-leaning sans faces, nothing formal. Rounded corners and `pill` buttons keep it approachable. The header is `transparent_on_hero`, sitting over the home `slideshow` and the photo `banner` that opens every inner page, then turning solid and `sticky` on scroll. Default spacing density with tight `trust-bar` strips; comfortable but not sparse.

## Adapting

- Pool, paving, fencing or roofing contractor: keep everything, swap the photos, rewrite the six `services` cards and the `steps`, and refill `projects` with your own jobs. The `comparison-slider` is the strongest proof section for any before-and-after trade.
- Interior painter or renovation builder: same structure; rename `portfolio` to "Recent work" and turn `news` into project diaries.
- Garden centre or nursery: drop the `comparison-slider`, turn the `services` `card-grid` into departments and use `projects` for show gardens or seasonal displays. It will not sell plants online.
- Cleaning or pest-control service: keep `services`, `about` and `contact`, drop `portfolio` entirely (little to show) and lean on `testimonials` and the `accordion` pages instead.
- Poor fit for businesses without visual work (consultancy, accountancy): the `slideshow`, `comparison-slider` and `project-showcase` have nothing to show and the preset loses its reason to exist. Also a poor fit for a shop with a catalogue; there is no product listing or pricing section.

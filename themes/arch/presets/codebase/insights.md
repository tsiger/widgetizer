# Codebase

## Suits

A small web or product engineering studio: a team that builds sites, dashboards and portals for clients and wins work through shipped projects and a transparent process. `index` opens on a `split-hero`, then a `logo-cloud`, an `image-text` on how the studio works, a `project-showcase` of four builds, a `testimonial-hero` on a dark band, an `icon-card-grid` of capabilities, a four-phase `steps` section and a closing `action-bar`. `services` goes deeper with an `icon-card-grid`, a `numbered-service-list` of specialties, a three-tier `pricing` table (ways of working, not fixed prices) and an `accordion`; `work` is a `project-showcase` with `testimonials`; `team` has a `profile-grid`, `key-figures` and a founding `timeline`; `contact` sets expectations with a `split-content` brief checklist, a `features-split` on what happens next and an `accordion`.

It seeds two collections: `projects` with five case studies (marketing site, grocery rebuild, analytics dashboard, clinic booking, membership portal) and `news` with five studio notes. Suitability is not functionality: "Start a project" links to `contact`, which gives an email; nothing is quoted or booked on the site, and the `pricing` table is a conversation starter.

## Look

Technical, warm and unfussy, a developer studio that is not trying to look like a startup landing page. The light surfaces are warm paper tones rather than pure white, highlight bands are near-black, and the accent is a terracotta-orange (lighter coral on dark) that feels handmade rather than corporate. Space Grotesk headings bring a monospace-adjacent, engineering character; Inter body keeps it plain. Everything is `sharp`, spacing is `default`. The header is solid in the dark `highlight-primary` scheme, sticky, with no contact lines, just the logo, nav and a CTA; inner pages open on `rich-text` or `image-text` titles, not photo banners.

## Adapting

- Design or branding studio: swap the `pricing` tiers for `numbered-service-list` disciplines, keep `project-showcase` and the `projects` collection, soften the accent.
- Freelance developer: collapse `team` into `about` with one `image-text`, keep the `steps` process and `pricing` (hourly, project, retainer).
- App or SaaS consultancy: turn `projects` into client outcomes, make the `icon-card-grid` platforms and stacks, keep `news` as engineering notes.
- Marketing or SEO agency: `key-figures` become results, `logo-cloud` stays, `pricing` becomes monthly plans.
- Poor fit for hospitality, retail or any walk-in business: there is no hours, menu or map structure, and the muted engineering palette would read as cold. Also a poor fit for a large agency wanting a glossy, animated home page; this preset is deliberately restrained.

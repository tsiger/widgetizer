# Ironform

## Suits

- A solo coach or very small team selling results by appointment: personal trainers, strength coaches, running or triathlon coaches, nutrition coaches, rehab and mobility specialists. The home leads with a dark photo `banner`, a `scrolling-text` motto strip, `key-figures` for the track record, a `numbered-service-list` of how training works and client `testimonials`.
- `training` is the offer page: `numbered-service-list` of what every programme covers, `steps` for how it works, an `image-text` and a "before you book" `accordion`. `results` is the proof page (`key-figures`, a `masonry-gallery` of client training shots, `testimonials` on a `highlight-primary` band). `about` is written for one person: an `image-text` portrait opener, a `timeline` of credentials and a `testimonial-hero`.
- Seeds `services` (programmes such as one-to-one coaching, a twelve-week transformation, a form check) and `news` (coaching notes, milestones). There is no `projects` collection.
- Suitability is not functionality: the "Book a Session" button and every `action-bar` lead to the `book` page, which is a `steps` walkthrough plus `accordion`, not a scheduler. Nothing is booked, paid or scheduled on the site.

## Look

Hard-edged and motivational. Warm off-white and putty surfaces for the standard flow, near-black `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands, a burnt-amber accent that glows brighter on dark. Oswald headings (condensed, bold, uppercase-friendly) over DM Sans body text: gym-poster energy with a readable sans. `sharp` corners and buttons, `compact` spacing density, so pages feel dense and punchy rather than airy. The header itself sits on `highlight-primary`, so it is dark on every page, and is `transparent_on_hero` over the large photo `banner` that opens the home, `training`, `results` and `book`; `about` opens with an `image-text` instead. Highlights do the heavy lifting: opener, one proof band, closing `action-bar`.

## Adapting

- Boxing, martial-arts or climbing coach: keep everything; rewrite `services` items as class or coaching formats and refill the `results` gallery.
- Physiotherapist or sports-rehab practitioner: rename `training` to "Treatment", soften the headlines, keep `steps` and the credentials `timeline`; drop `key-figures` that read as promises.
- Nutritionist or online coach: `training` becomes plans, `results` becomes client stories (use `testimonials` more than the gallery), `book` stays as the enquiry walkthrough.
- Music teacher, tutor or driving instructor: same solo-expert shape; `services` as lesson types, `results` as student outcomes, `book` as how lessons start.
- Poor fit for a multi-site gym or studio with timetables and memberships: there is no `class-schedule` or `pricing` section here, and the `about` page is built around one person. Also a poor fit for soft, calm or luxury brands; the type and `compact` rhythm are deliberately aggressive.

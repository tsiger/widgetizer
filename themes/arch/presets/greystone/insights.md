# Greystone

## Suits

- Professional practices that sell judgement and a track record: law firms first, then notaries, mediators, financial advisers, insurance brokers and expert consultancies. Trust is built through credentials and outcomes, not photos.
- The home opens with a short photo `banner` headline, a `trust-bar` of credentials, a `features-split` of practice areas, `key-figures` (years, cases, recoveries) and client `testimonials`, closing with a consultation `action-bar`. The `services` page ("Practice Areas") pairs a `card-grid` with a `numbered-cards` process; the `work` page ("Case Results") is a `project-showcase` of matters with `key-figures` on a `highlight-primary` band.
- The `about` page carries the institutional story: `timeline` of firm milestones, `profile-grid` of attorneys, `accordion` on approach. `contact` is deliberately plain: a `map` with office details and a frequently-asked `accordion`, no opener.
- Seeds only `news` (firm announcements, clinics, articles); the case results are written into the page, not a collection.
- Suitability is not functionality: "Free Consultation" and every `action-bar` point to `contact`; there is no booking, intake form or client portal.

## Look

Established and reserved. Warm stone neutrals (off-white and pale greige surfaces) with near-black `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands; a bronze accent on light surfaces turns to antique gold on dark ones. Libre Baskerville headings, slightly enlarged, over Outfit body text: classic serif authority with a clean modern sans. `sharp` corners, default spacing. The header is solid (`transparent_on_hero` off) and `sticky`, with phone and street address beside the logo; the home opener is a medium photo `banner`, while `services` and `work` open with text-only banners and `contact` with no banner at all. Highlights are used sparingly and always for the same thing: the closing call to action and the key proof.

## Adapting

- Accountancy, tax or wealth advisers: rename `services` to "Services", turn `work` into "Client stories" (keep `project-showcase`, soften the `key-figures` to savings or clients served) and keep `about` as is.
- Architecture or engineering consultancy: `work` becomes the portfolio; add images to the `project-showcase` and consider seeding `projects` to grow it.
- Medical or dental specialist practice: `services` becomes treatments, `profile-grid` the clinicians, `timeline` the practice history; replace "case results" with patient `testimonials` and drop the figures that would read as claims.
- Insurance or mortgage broker: keep the home and `services`; drop `work` or repurpose it for "How we helped".
- Poor fit for anything playful, retail or hospitality: the palette and serif headings read as formal, and there is no gallery, pricing or product section. Also weak for a solo practitioner with one service; the practice-area grids will look empty.

# Little Oaks

## Suits

- Childcare and early education: daycares, preschools, nurseries, after-school clubs, and with light changes any family-facing programme business (music school for children, swim school, summer camp). Parents want to see programmes, a typical day, prices and the people, in that order, and the pages are built that way.
- The home opens with a `split-hero` (headline beside a photo), then a `trust-bar` of reassurances, a `card-grid` of programmes by age group, an `image-text` about the approach, `key-figures` and parent `testimonials`. `programs` goes deeper with a `checkerboard` describing each programme and a `schedule-table` showing a sample daily rhythm. `tuition` is the decision page: `pricing` by programme, enrollment `steps` and an `accordion` of fee questions.
- `about` is values and people (`icon-card-grid`, `profile-grid`, `testimonials`); `contact` is a `map` and a "before you come in" `accordion`.
- Seeds `news` (open mornings, forest-school days, staff introductions). No `projects` or `services`.
- Suitability is not functionality: "Schedule a Tour" and every `action-bar` link to `contact`; there is no enrollment form, waiting list or payment, and the tuition figures are text the author keeps current.

## Look

Warm, soft and reassuring. Cream-white and light biscuit surfaces, a muted leaf-green accent, charcoal (not black) `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands so even the dark sections stay gentle. Fredoka headings (rounded, chubby, child-friendly without being cartoonish) over Nunito body text, also rounded. `rounded` corners and `pill` buttons throughout; default spacing. The header is solid (`transparent_on_hero` off), `full_width` and `sticky`, showing phone and address beside the logo; because the header never goes transparent, inner pages open with simple text `banner` titles on `highlight-primary` rather than photos. Closing `action-bar` sections use small spacing so they feel like a footer-adjacent nudge rather than a big dark block.

## Adapting

- After-school club, holiday camp or play centre: rename `programs` to "Sessions", turn the `schedule-table` into the weekly timetable and `pricing` into session passes.
- Children's music, dance, art or swim school: `programs` as classes by age, `tuition` as term fees, `profile-grid` as teachers; keep the parent `testimonials`.
- Tutoring centre or language school for children: same structure; the `steps` on `tuition` become "how to enrol" and `news` becomes term dates and results.
- Senior day-care or community centre: the age-group `card-grid` becomes activity strands and the `schedule-table` the daily programme; the palette already reads as caring rather than childish.
- Poor fit for premium, minimal or corporate brands: the rounded type and pastel warmth are deliberately cosy. Also a poor fit for a business whose draw is a venue or a portfolio; there is no gallery section and no photo opener beyond the home.

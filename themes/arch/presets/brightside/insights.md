# Brightside

## Suits

A family or general dental practice, and by extension any appointment-based clinic where the visitor is nervous and checking credentials. `index` opens on a `banner`, then a `trust-bar` of accreditations, an `icon-card-grid` of treatments, a before-and-after `comparison-slider`, an `image-text` introducing the lead dentist, `testimonials` and a booking `action-bar`. `services` adds `numbered-cards` for what to expect, an `image-text` on technology and a `rich-text` on insurance and payment; `new-patients` walks through the first visit with `steps` and an `accordion`; `smile-gallery` is a `comparison-slider` and a `gallery` of results; `about` has `profile-grid` for the dentists and an office `gallery`; `contact` gives a `schedule-table` of hours and a `map`.

It seeds two collections: `services` with five treatments (check-up, whitening, fillings, children's dentistry, emergency care, each with `icon`, `summary` and `description`) and `news` with five practice updates. Suitability is not functionality: the "Book Appointment" header button links to `contact`; no appointment is booked and no insurance is verified.

## Look

Clinical but warm, bright and reassuring. White primary surfaces, a pale sky-blue secondary band, deep navy `highlight-primary` and `highlight-secondary` bands, and a clear mid-blue accent (lighter blue on dark). Plus Jakarta Sans headings and Inter body make an all-sans, geometric, trustworthy pairing with no decorative serif. Corners are `sharp` but buttons are `pill`, which softens the calls to action. The header is solid, in the dark `highlight-secondary` scheme, with phone and address as contact lines and `transparent_on_hero` off; every page opens on a `highlight-primary` `banner`. Spacing is `default`.

## Adapting

- Physiotherapy, chiropractic or optometry clinic: rename treatments in the `services` collection and `icon-card-grid`, replace the `smile-gallery` page with a results or facilities `gallery`, keep `new-patients` as is.
- Veterinary clinic: `profile-grid` becomes the vets, the `comparison-slider` can go, `schedule-table` gains emergency hours.
- Dermatology or cosmetic clinic: the `comparison-slider` and `smile-gallery` are the strongest assets; keep them, add `pricing` to `services`.
- Medical or specialist practice: drop `smile-gallery`, keep the insurance `rich-text`, turn `news` into patient information articles.
- Poor fit for restaurants, shops or creative studios: the structure assumes a nervous first-time patient and a credentials-led trust story. Also a poor fit if you want dark, moody photography; the palette is built to feel clean and bright.

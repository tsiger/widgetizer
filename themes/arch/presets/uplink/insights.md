# Uplink

## Suits

- A managed IT support provider selling a monthly retainer to small and mid-sized businesses. The `index` page is a B2B sales page: a `split-hero` on a light surface, a `trust-bar`, a `numbered-service-list` of five pillars, a `card-grid` of client types, a `logo-cloud` of supported teams, a `highlight-secondary` `testimonial-hero`, `steps` for a 60-day handover, and a closing `banner` with `height` set to `auto` as the call to action.
- `pricing` is the decision page: a three-tier `pricing` grid, a `numbered-service-list` of what every tier includes, a `highlight-secondary` `testimonial-slider` and a pre-signing `accordion`. Prices are published; nothing is purchased or signed on the site, and the free assessment is requested by phone or email.
- `services` uses `image-tabs` to walk through the managed stack, then `numbered-service-list`, another `logo-cloud` and an `accordion`. `about` adds `image-text`, a `profile-grid` of engineers, a vendor `logo-cloud`, a commitments `split-content` and a `highlight-primary` `testimonial-hero`.
- Closing calls to action are `banner` sections rather than `action-bar`, which gives the preset a boxier, more corporate finish than the trade and hospitality presets.
- `contact` opens with a `small` photo `banner`, then `contact-details` in a `first-featured` layout and an `accordion`. Seeds `news` with five posts used as security advice, helpdesk announcements and a company anniversary. No `projects` or `services` items.

## Look

Precise, technical and cool: near-white grey surfaces, a cyan-teal accent, and near-black highlight bands where the accent turns to a bright electric cyan. Both fonts are sans serif: a crisp grotesque at medium weight (Chivo) for headings over a tight neutral body (Inter Tight), reading as engineering rather than marketing. The header is `sticky`, `full_width`, `transparent_on_hero` with `center_nav`, but the home page opens with a `split-hero` on `standard-primary`, so the header stays solid there and floats only over the `small` photo `banner` openers on inner pages. `spacing_density` is `default`, corners `slightly-rounded`, buttons `auto`: tidy, structured, lots of lists and logos.

## Adapting

- Cybersecurity consultancy, cloud or DevOps provider: keep everything; rewrite the `pricing` tiers and the `image-tabs` stack, and let the `logo-cloud` carry certifications instead of clients.
- Accounting, payroll or bookkeeping firm on a monthly fee: the retainer `pricing`, the `steps` handover and the `testimonial-hero` proof translate directly; drop the vendor `logo-cloud`.
- Marketing or SEO agency: `services` becomes service lines in `image-tabs`, `pricing` becomes retainers, and `news` becomes insights.
- Poor fit for consumer-facing local businesses, since there is no `map`, no `gallery` and no opening-hours framing beyond the header, and for a studio that wants warmth and photography; the look is intentionally cool and text-and-logo led.

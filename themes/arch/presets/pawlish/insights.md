# Pawlish

## Suits

- Appointment-based pet-care businesses: dog grooming salons, cat groomers, mobile groomers, dog daycare, pet spas. The tone is reassuring and practical, written for a nervous owner rather than a bargain hunter.
- The home opens with a photo `banner`, an `image-text` with a numbered list of what makes the salon calm, an `icon-card-grid` of six services on a `highlight-primary` band, a before-and-after `comparison-slider` and a `testimonial-slider`. `services` is price-led: a `pricing` section by size bracket, a `bento-grid` of approaches per coat, an inclusions `icon-card-grid` and a pricing `accordion`. `first-visit` is the preset's signature: six `steps` from drop-off to pickup, a `checkerboard`, what to bring, an `image-callout` and a long `accordion` of first-timer questions.
- `groomers` is the team (`image-text`, `profile-grid`, promises in an `icon-card-grid`, a `testimonial-hero`); `gallery` is one `masonry-gallery`; `contact` combines an `image-callout`, `contact-details` and a `map`.
- Seeds `services` (bath and brush, full groom, de-shedding, nail trim, puppy intro) and `news` (clinics, care tips, rescue days).
- Suitability is not functionality: "Book a groom" and every `action-bar` lead to `contact`, where the owner is told to call or email; there is no online booking, deposit or price calculator. Prices are text the author maintains.

## Look

Bold, warm and cheerful, the least neutral of the Arch presets. Standard surfaces are peach and apricot rather than white, highlight bands are deep plum (`highlight-primary`) and a lighter aubergine (`highlight-secondary`), the accent is raspberry on light surfaces and mustard yellow on dark. Fraunces headings (a soft, slightly quirky serif at a medium weight) over Karla body text, a plain humanist sans. `slightly-rounded` corners, default spacing. The header is `transparent_on_hero` over the photo `banner` that opens every page, with phone and opening days beside the logo. Highlights are used more freely than usual: two bands on most pages, always one of them the closing `action-bar`, which uses small spacing so it reads as a short sign-off.

## Adapting

- Cat groomer, mobile grooming van or pet spa: keep everything; rewrite `services` items and the size brackets in `pricing`, and refill the `comparison-slider` and `gallery`.
- Dog daycare, boarding or walking: `services` becomes day and overnight options, `first-visit` becomes the trial-day walkthrough, the `comparison-slider` is dropped and the `gallery` carries the play yard.
- Children's hair salon, barber or nail studio: same anxious-first-visit logic and price brackets; `first-visit` becomes "Your first appointment".
- Poor fit for luxury, minimal or masculine brands; the peach-and-plum palette is intentionally sweet and cannot be made quiet without changing every colour. Also a poor fit for a shop that wants to sell products, since there is no product catalogue and `pricing` is built for service tiers.

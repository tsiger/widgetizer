# Stillpoint

## Suits

- A yoga or movement studio that runs a weekly timetable and wants new students to feel welcome before they arrive. The `index` page is calm and sequential: a `medium` photo `banner`, a `scrolling-text` strip, a `rich-text` statement, an `icon-card-grid` of class styles, an `image-text` about the space, `steps` for getting started, a `trust-bar` and a `highlight-primary` `action-bar` offering the first class free.
- `classes` describes each practice through an `icon-card-grid`, sets expectations with `features-split`, carries pricing in a `highlight-primary` `rich-text` band, introduces a lead teacher with `image-text` and answers questions in an `accordion`. `schedule` presents the weekly timetable as a `card-grid` with an `image-text` for beginners. Suitability is not functionality: the timetable is a static page, so classes are not booked and memberships are not sold on the site.
- `about` builds the community feel with `image-text`, `features-split` values, a `profile-grid` of instructors and a `gallery` of the room. `gallery` and `contact` are simple supporting pages with a `map` and an `accordion`.
- Seeds `news` with five posts used as studio notes: a new class, a workshop, teacher training, a breathing piece. No `projects` or `services` items.
- The header carries no phone or hours; contact lives in the footer, which suits a studio that communicates by email and notices.

## Look

Warm, soft and unhurried: off-white and pale sand surfaces, a terracotta-clay accent, and deep brown highlight bands where the accent lightens to a peach-clay. Headings are a light-weight serif (Cormorant Garamond at regular weight) over a humanist sans (Karla), which feels gentle and personal. The header is `sticky`, `full_width` and `center_nav` with `transparent_on_hero` off, so it stays a solid bar on every page, including over the home `banner`; inner pages open with a `small` photo `banner`. `spacing_density` is `airy`, corners `rounded`, buttons `pill`: the loosest, most breathing-room layout in the catalogue, matched to the subject.

## Adapting

- Pilates, barre or dance studio: keep every page; rename the `icon-card-grid` classes and the `card-grid` timetable, and keep the `steps` onboarding.
- Meditation centre or wellness retreat: turn `schedule` into a programme page, use `features-split` on `classes` for retreat formats, and let `news` carry upcoming retreats.
- Martial arts or climbing gym: the timetable `card-grid`, the instructor `profile-grid` and the first-class-free `action-bar` translate directly; swap the palette mood through the images rather than the structure.
- Music, art or language school: `classes` becomes courses, `schedule` becomes term dates, and `gallery` shows student work.
- Poor fit for a business that needs online booking or payments, since nothing here transacts, and for a high-energy fitness brand that wants bold type and tight spacing; the whole look is quiet by design.

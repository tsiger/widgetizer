# Hearthstone

## Suits

- Small hospitality: boutique hotels, B&Bs, guesthouses, villas and agritourism stays with a handful of rooms and a personal host. Every page opens with a wide photo `banner` and the home is built from `split-content` storytelling, a `bento-grid` of three rooms, an `icon-list` of what every stay includes, a `testimonials` carousel and a `masonry-gallery`.
- The `rooms` page lists accommodation in a `card-grid` with starting rates, and `room-jasmine` is a worked example of a single-room page (gallery, `icon-list` of amenities, "before you book" `accordion`); duplicate it for each room. `nearby` sells the location with an `icon-card-grid` of places, a `map` and a `priced-list` of extras the host arranges.
- `about` ("The Villa") is history and hosts: `timeline` and `team-highlight`. `contact` is a letter-style `split-content` with `contact-details`, `map` and `accordion`.
- Seeds `news` (season openings, kitchen stories, events). No `projects` or `services`.
- Suitability is not functionality: "Check availability" and every `action-bar` lead to `contact`; the site has no booking engine, calendar or payment. Rates on the cards are text the author maintains.

## Look

Calm, coastal and unhurried. Near-white and pale blue-grey surfaces, deep navy `highlight-primary` bands, a clear blue accent and coral rating stars; cool without being cold. Cormorant Garamond headings at a light weight over Plus Jakarta Sans body text: elegant serif, contemporary sans. `airy` spacing density, `slightly-rounded` corners and a `full_width` header that is `transparent_on_hero` over the large home `banner` and the smaller photo banners on every inner page. Highlights are reserved for the opener and the closing `action-bar`; the middle of each page alternates `standard-primary` and `standard-secondary` banding.

## Adapting

- Holiday rental or single villa: merge `rooms` into one `room-jasmine`-style page, keep `nearby` and `about`, and turn the `priced-list` into optional services.
- Restaurant with rooms or a wine estate: `rooms` stays, `nearby` becomes "The table" or "The vineyard" with the `priced-list` as a tasting or set-menu list; it describes the menu, it does not take orders.
- Retreat or wellness centre: `rooms` becomes programmes, `room-jasmine` a single retreat page, `nearby` the setting; keep the `testimonials` carousel.
- Wedding or event venue: `rooms` becomes spaces, the `masonry-gallery` carries past events, `news` becomes real weddings.
- Poor fit for a large hotel with dozens of room types, dynamic pricing or a reservation system; every room is a hand-made page. Also a poor fit for a business without strong photography, since every opener is a photo and the gallery sections are the proof.

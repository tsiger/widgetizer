# Everafter

## Suits

A wedding planning studio: a small team selling full planning, partial planning and day-of coordination to couples making an emotional, high-ticket, once-only decision. `index` opens on a full-width photo `banner`, then a `trust-bar` of press features, an `image-text` on the studio's approach, an `icon-card-grid` of three ways to work together, a `masonry-gallery` of weddings, a `testimonial-hero` on a dark band, a `steps` section on how an engagement unfolds and a closing `action-bar`. `services` is the decision page: a three-tier `pricing` table of packages, a full-width `image`, a `numbered-service-list` of what the work involves and an `accordion`. `portfolio` is a `masonry-gallery` with `testimonials`; `about` has `key-figures`, a studio `timeline`, a `profile-grid` of four planners and a second `trust-bar`; `contact` sets expectations with a `split-content` enquiry checklist, a `features-split` on what happens next and an `accordion`.

It seeds two collections: `projects` with five real weddings (barn, marquee, registry office, coastal two-day, winter city hall) with `client` and `year`, and `news` with five studio posts. Suitability is not functionality: "Inquire" links to `contact`, which gives an email; nothing is booked and the `pricing` tiers are starting points, not a checkout.

## Look

Romantic, luxurious and calm, closer to a fashion magazine than a bridal fair. Light surfaces are warm, sun-faded linen tones, highlight bands are near-black espresso, and the accent is a terracotta rose on light with antique gold on dark. Bodoni Moda gives high-contrast, fashion-editorial headings; Work Sans body is neutral and modern. Corners are `slightly-rounded`, `spacing_density` is `airy`, so everything feels unhurried. The header is `transparent_on_hero`, sticky, with no contact lines, floating over the home photo; inner pages open with `rich-text`, `image-text` or `split-content` titles instead of banners so the header stays solid and readable.

## Adapting

- Event planner or corporate events agency: rename `portfolio` to Events, turn `projects` items into launches and galas, replace the couple's `testimonial-hero` quote with a client one.
- Wedding photographer or videographer: drop the `pricing` tiers to two, keep both `masonry-gallery` sections, make `services` about coverage hours.
- Florist or wedding stylist: `projects` become installations, the `icon-card-grid` becomes services (bouquets, ceremony, reception), keep the press `trust-bar`.
- Boutique venue: `services` becomes hire packages, `about` carries the venue history via `timeline`, add a `map` to `contact`.
- Poor fit for anything fast, cheap or transactional (a shop, a takeaway, a repair service): the `airy` rhythm and editorial serif read as expensive and slow by design. Poor fit for a solo planner who wants a chatty first-person voice; this is a studio with four named people.

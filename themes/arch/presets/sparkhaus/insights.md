# Sparkhaus

## Suits

- A residential cleaning service sold on trust and speed of quoting. The `index` page is conversion-led: a `large` photo `banner`, a `trust-bar` of guarantees, a before-and-after `comparison-slider`, a `card-grid` of four service types, `numbered-cards` for the quote-to-clean process, a `highlight-primary` `key-figures` band, `testimonials` in a `carousel` and a closing `action-bar`.
- `services` is the pricing page: a `pricing` section that rewards booking on a cadence (weekly, fortnightly, monthly), a `priced-list` of a-la-carte extras, an `icon-list` of what every clean includes with no upsell, and an `accordion` of booking questions. Prices are shown and compared; nothing is booked or paid for on the site.
- `about` is about who enters your home: `image-text`, an `image-callout` on licensing and insurance, a `profile-grid` of the team, `key-figures` and a `timeline`. It opens on `image-text`, not a hero.
- `contact` is framed as a quick quote: `image-text`, an `icon-list` of the five details to send, a `features-split` on what happens next, a `map` of the service area and an `accordion`. A form would only receive submissions on Widgetizer Hosting; otherwise the phone and email in the header and footer do the work.
- Seeds `news` with five posts used as offers, team introductions and cleaning tips. No `projects` or `services` items.

## Look

Fresh, clean and reassuring: cool blue-white surfaces with a soft slate-blue banding tone, a leafy green accent, and deep navy highlight bands that keep the same green. Both fonts are sans serif: a bold geometric heading face (Manrope) over a friendly geometric body (DM Sans), so the type feels modern and approachable rather than corporate. The header is `sticky`, `full_width` and `transparent_on_hero`, but only `index` opens on a photo `banner`; the other pages open on `image-text` or `rich-text`, so the header is solid there. `spacing_density` is `default`, corners `slightly-rounded`, buttons `pill`: tidy and soft-edged, like a well-run service.

## Adapting

- Window cleaning, carpet cleaning or pressure washing: keep everything; the `comparison-slider` is made for before-and-after shots, and the `pricing` cadence tiers become per-visit or seasonal plans.
- Lawn care, gardening or pool maintenance: same cadence logic in `pricing`; swap the `card-grid` services and let `news` carry seasonal reminders.
- Laundry or dry-cleaning pickup, home organising, pet sitting or dog walking: recurring plans in `pricing`, the trust `image-callout` on `about` for vetting and insurance, and the quick-quote `contact` page translate directly.
- Commercial or office cleaning: reframe `pricing` around contract sizes and move the `profile-grid` lower, since buyers care more about process than faces.
- Poor fit for a one-off, high-ticket service such as renovation or landscaping design, where there is no cadence to price and a portfolio matters more than a `comparison-slider`. Also a poor fit for a brand that wants a dark or luxurious feel; the palette is deliberately bright and light.
